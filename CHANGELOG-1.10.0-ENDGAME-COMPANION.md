# VoidedClient 1.10.0 — Endgame Companion extension

- Keeps the public client version at 1.10.0.
- Adds an Endgame tab to the unified Companion.
- Displays Ascension, Endgame Marks, daily/lifetime trial progress, tiered Gauntlet clears, active Gauntlet state and overall completion.
- Adds `endgame-companion-v1` and the `ENDGAME_COMPANION` packet parser.
- Fabric and Forge are updated together.
