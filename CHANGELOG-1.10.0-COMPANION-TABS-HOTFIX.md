# VoidedClient 1.10.0 - Unified Companion Tabs Hotfix

- Exploration now opens inside the unified Companion dashboard instead of a separate screen.
- The daily exploration contract is a sub-view inside the Explore tab.
- RPG Character now opens inside the unified Companion dashboard instead of a separate RPG screen.
- RPG stat allocation, refresh, and respec controls are available directly from the RPG tab.
- Explore and RPG now receive the same active-tab indicator as the other Companion sections.
- The J RPG keybind and K exploration keybind route into their matching Companion tabs.
- Added the missing Forge RPG stats receiver so Forge matches Fabric behavior.
- Corrected the Fabric/Forge HELLO-reported client version to 1.10.0.
- Corrected the stale Gradle compatibility error text to 1.10.0.
