# VoidedClient 1.8.1

- Adds the native Exploration Journal on Fabric and Forge, opened with K.
- Displays discovered custom mobs in colour with their server-owned kill totals.
- Keeps undiscovered mobs blacked out and hides their names.
- Sends normalized WASD input with yaw/pitch for forward, backward, sideways and diagonal Dash movement.
- Keeps backward and sideways Dash horizontal while retaining pitch for forward Dash.
- Preserves compatibility with the VoidedClient 1.8.0 look-only Dash payload.
